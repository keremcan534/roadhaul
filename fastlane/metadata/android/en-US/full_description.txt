Start with one truck and your name on the door. Take a contract, load the cargo, drive it across a living country and deliver it on time: every job pays, and every credit builds your company.

DRIVE
• A heavy truck that feels heavy: it leans into the bends, and if you take a corner too fast it tips onto two wheels and rolls over.
• Twelve trucks: box trucks, refrigerated trucks, flatbeds, a tanker, a tipper, and tractor units that pull semi-trailers.
• Steer with the on-screen wheel, by tilting your phone, or with left and right buttons.
• Five cameras, from the chase view to the cabin with its dashboard, mirrors and wipers.
• GPS navigation shows the next turn, the distance by road and your arrival time.
• Pick 30 or 60 frames a second, or as many as your screen shows: 90 and 120 Hz screens included.

TWO REGIONS
• North Valley: seven towns, thirteen villages, farms, deep forests, a river with bridges, a fishing port, a lighthouse on the point and a mountain pass under snowy peaks.
• Metropol, across the sea: towers, motorways and a bridge over the strait. Take the ferry from Gull Point once your company has grown.
• Cars, buses, taxis and lorries on the roads, people on the pavements, traffic lights, road signs and direction boards.
• Day and night, rain, snow, thunderstorms, morning mist, rainbows, the four seasons, the northern lights and shooting stars.
• Speed cameras, red light cameras and road works. Stop for a stranded driver, or ride along with a convoy.
• Knock over lamps and signs and send bins flying, then pay for the repairs.

BUILD A COMPANY
• Buy trucks new or used, paint them, give them a design and your own lettering, and upgrade the engine, brakes, tyres, suspension and fuel tank.
• Hire drivers and send them out: your fleet earns money while you drive, and even while you are away.
• Race rival companies for tenders and in the weekly cup, take the lead in each city and buy your rivals out.
• Build a workshop, a fuel depot, a truck yard and more at your head office, and research better ways to run it.

AND MORE
• New contracts every day, daily quests, a daily reward and weekly events with bonus pay.
• Crates to earn and open: credits, boosters, parts and new paints.
• The Road Pass: rewards as you drive, with an optional Plus track of extra colours.
• Photo mode: move round your truck, pick a look and save the picture.
• Goals, achievements, your career's record and the cab radio.
• Plays offline. Your progress is saved on your phone, and you can back it up to a file.
• Nothing for sale speeds the game up.
• Available in 10 languages.
