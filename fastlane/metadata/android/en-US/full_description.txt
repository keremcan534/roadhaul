Start with one truck and your name on the door. Take a contract, load the cargo, drive it across a living countryside and deliver it on time: every job pays, and every credit builds your company.

DRIVE
• A heavy truck that feels heavy: it leans into the bends, and if you take a corner too fast it tips onto two wheels and rolls over.
• Steer with the on-screen wheel, by tilting your phone, or with left and right buttons.
• Five cameras, from the chase view to the cabin with its dashboard, mirrors and wipers.
• GPS navigation shows the next turn, the distance by road and your arrival time.

A LIVING WORLD
• Four cities, thirteen villages, farms, forests, a river with bridges and a harbour by the sea.
• Cars, buses and lorries on the roads, people on the pavements, road signs and direction boards.
• Day and night, rain, snow, thunderstorms, morning mist, rainbows and the four seasons.
• Knock over lamps and signs and send bins flying, then pay for the repairs.

BUILD A COMPANY
• Buy new trucks, paint them and upgrade the engine, brakes, tyres, suspension and fuel tank.
• Hire drivers and send them out: your fleet earns money while you drive, and even while you are away.
• Race rival companies for tenders, take the lead in each city and buy your rivals out.
• Build a workshop, a fuel depot, a truck yard and more at your head office.

AND MORE
• New contracts every day and weekly events with bonus pay.
• Plays offline. Your progress is saved on your phone.
• Available in 10 languages.
